MSME Day Cavite Workshop: Grateful for the Connections

Congratulations to CAITO (Calabarzon Alliance of Independent Tour Operators, Inc.) on a successful MSME Day: Cavite Workshop—organized in partnership with PLDT Enterprise.

On August 11, Nitro Tech Media was glad to be part of the day at Twin Lakes Hotel Tagaytay. Thank you to CAITO for having us. It was a pleasure meeting each guest, learning more about their businesses, and sharing real conversations throughout the workshop.

Events like this aren't about pitching all day—they're about listening, connecting, and finding where technology can actually help a local business grow.

Why we showed up

MSME Day brings together business owners, entrepreneurs, and partners who are serious about improving how they operate. For a Cavite-based team like ours, that's home ground. We were there to talk about digital solutions—websites, systems, and tools that strengthen presence and day-to-day operations—not as abstract "tech," but as practical support for real businesses.

Having a booth meant we got to hear firsthand what owners are dealing with: scattered inquiries, manual processes, and the gap between wanting to grow digitally and knowing where to start. Those conversations matter more than any brochure we hand out.

What we're taking with us

We truly appreciate every interaction, conversation, and connection from the day. To everyone who stopped by our booth and took the time to connect—thank you. Hearing about your businesses up close is exactly why we keep showing up at events like this.

Here's to more successful gatherings, stronger partnerships with organizations like CAITO, and more opportunities to build with the MSME community in Cavite and beyond.
