Supporting BetterTreceMartires.org — Civic Tech for Transparent Governance

Let's welcome BetterTreceMartires.org—a new civic technology initiative for transparent governance and a more empowered Trece Martires.

Nitro Tech Media is proud to support the launch of this open-source platform, spearheaded by Arnel Baylon as part of the broader BetterGovPH movement. The goal is simple and important: make public information more accessible, and encourage transparency, accountability, and citizen engagement where people actually live.

Technology can be more than a tool for business innovation—it can inform communities, spark better conversations, and help citizens take a more active role in the future they deserve.

How we helped

Our support was practical. We sponsored the domain so BetterTreceMartires.org has a clear, lasting home online, and we helped give the project exposure—because a civic platform only matters if people know it exists and can find it.

BetterTreceMartires.org sits in the BetterGovPH ecosystem of community-led LGU portals: modern, usable front doors to services, local data, and public information that too often stays buried in hard-to-navigate official sites. It doesn't replace government systems—it complements them by putting clarity and access first.

Why this matters to us

We're based in Cavite. Supporting an open-source project built for Trece Martires City isn't abstract civic branding—it's backing neighbors who are using tech to make governance easier to understand and engage with.

Together, let's keep backing initiatives that promote accessible information, transparent governance, and meaningful participation. Every step toward a more informed community is a step toward better governance for all.

Proud to stand with Arnel Baylon, BetterGovPH, and everyone building in the open for the public good.

— Bryan
