CAITO 3rd GMM: Officially Joining the Family

On August 28, Nitro Tech Media attended the 3rd General Membership Meeting of CAITO (Calabarzon Alliance of Independent Tour Operators, Inc.) at Enchanted Kingdom in Santa Rosa, Laguna—and we left as an official member.

Members and guests gathered to connect, collaborate, and dig into the day's theme: “The Importance of Theme Parks in Tourism That Magnets Local Tourists.” Being in that room—after months of working alongside CAITO on digital work and events—felt like the right next step, not just another calendar invite.

From sponsor and builder to member: the oath-taking made official what we've been building toward with CAITO all along.

Why this milestone matters

This year we've already stood with CAITO at CTBEX 2026, joined them for MSME Day Cavite, and shipped caito.org.ph. Becoming a member locks that partnership into the organization itself—not only as a vendor or booth partner, but as part of the alliance shaping tourism conversations in CALABARZON.

The GMM was full of the usual good stuff: meaningful conversations, new faces, and shared milestones. For us, the oath-taking was the moment that stuck—certificates in hand, PLDT and fellow members in the room, and a clear commitment to keep showing up.

Looking ahead

Here's to stronger connections, real collaborations, and more chances to support initiatives that help Philippine tourism grow—especially where theme parks, local travel, and digital tools meet.

Proud to be part of the CAITO family.
